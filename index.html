    How the magic works
    
    
      
      
    1. Knock. Séance knocks on the live URL. If nothing answers, the site is declared dead.
      2. 
      
    2. Consult the Archive. It asks the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine for the nearest surviving snapshot and one capture per year of the site's life.
      3. 
      
    3. Raise the ghost. The snapshot is shown in the viewer, exactly as the site looked when it was alive.
      4. 
    
    
    
    No accounts, no tracking, no servers of our own. All data comes from the public Internet Archive APIs (archive.org / web.archive.org). Snapshots belong to the Internet Archive and the original sites.
