# Demo video script — Séance (< 3 minutes)

Target: under 3:00, screen recording with voiceover, no music, no
third-party trademarks beyond the archived sites themselves (fair-use
tribute). Record at 1080p.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook
Show the Séance homepage. Voiceover: "Every dead link leaves a ghost in
the Internet Archive. Séance is a table where you summon it."
Type `geocities.com`, hit Summon.

## 0:20–0:50 — The autopsy
The verdict card appears: "GEOCITIES.COM IS DEAD — last ghost seen
2013-07-18." Voiceover: "Séance knocks on the live address first. Nothing
answers — so it asks the Archive, which kept the site's soul."

## 0:50–1:30 — The timeline + apparition
Click 1999 on the timeline, then 2005. The archived GeoCities page loads
in the viewer. Voiceover: "One snapshot per year. Pick a year, and the
site is alive again — exactly as it looked when it was."

## 1:30–2:00 — The Graveyard
Scroll to the Graveyard, click the Vine headstone. Its autopsy appears.
Voiceover: "Or skip the typing — the Graveyard holds twelve famous ghosts,
one click each."

## 2:00–2:30 — The obituary
Scroll to the obituary, click "Copy obituary". Voiceover: "Every séance
ends with an obituary. Copy it, share it, lay the site to rest."

## 2:30–2:55 — The build (Kiro)
Quick scroll through the repo: `.kiro/specs` (requirements, design,
tasks), `.kiro/steering`, hooks. Voiceover: "Built spec-first with Kiro's
workflow — requirements, design, tasks, steering docs and hooks, all in
`.kiro`. Zero backend, zero cost, only the Internet Archive's public APIs."

## 2:55–3:00 — Close
Back on the homepage. "Séance — resurrect the dead web. Built for
Kiroween 2026." End card with the GitHub + live-app URLs.
